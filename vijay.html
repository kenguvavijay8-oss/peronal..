Characteristics of Exotic Animals

  • They have unusual physical features (colors, patterns, horns, tails, etc.)
  • Many are found only in specific regions of the world.
  • Most exotic animals are endangered or vulnerable due to habitat loss
  • Some have unique behaviors, such as rare hunting, mating, or survival habits.
