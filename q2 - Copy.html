BIRDS

Birds are warm-blooded, egg-laying vertebrates that belong to the class Aves. They are characterized by feathers, beaks, lightweight skeletons, and the ability to fly (though some birds do not fly)

SPIX MACAW

Overview

The Spix’s Macaw (Cyanopsitta spixii), also called the Little Blue Macaw, is a small bright-blue parrot native to northeastern Brazil. It is famous for its extreme rarity and conservation story—declared extinct in the wild around 2000 and the focus of intensive captive breeding and reintroduction efforts.

1. Scientific Classification

  • Kingdom: Animalia
  • Phylum: Chordata
  • Class: Aves
  • Order: Psittaciformes
  • Family: Psittacidae
  • Genus: Cyanopsitta
  • Species:Cyanopsitta spixii

2. Physical Characteristics

Spix’s Macaws are entirely blue with lighter underparts, a greyish-black strong curved beak, and long narrow tail feathers. Typical size is55–60 cm in length and weight around 300–400

3. Habitat

Historically found in dry tropical forests and gallery woodlands along rivers in northeastern Brazil. They depend heavily on caraibeira trees (Tabebuia aurea) for food (seeds) and nesting. Habitat loss and the removal of these trees played a major role in their decline.

Specialities of the Spix’s Macaw

  • One of the Rarest Birds on Earth: Once declared extinct in the wild, it now survives mainly through global captive breeding programs.
  • Completely Blue Plumage: It is the only macaw with an entirely bright blue body, making it visually unique.
  • Strong Pair Bonding: Spix’s macaws usually mate for life and form very strong bonds with their partners.
  • Symbol of Global Conservation: Its dramatic survival and reintroduction story made it an international symbol of wildlife protection.
  • Famous in Popular Culture: Gained worldwide recognition after being featured in the animated movie "Rio."

PHILIPPINE EAGLE

1. Overview

The Philippine Eagle (Pithecophaga jefferyi) is one of the world’s rarest, largest, and most powerful birds of prey. Endemic to the Philippines, it is known for its massive size, shaggy crest of feathers, and remarkable hunting abilities. Due to severe habitat loss, fewer than 400 individuals remain in the wild.

1. Scientific Classification

  • Kingdom: Animalia
  • Phylum: Chordata
  • Class: Aves
  • Order: Accipitriformes
  • Family: Accipitridae
  • Genus: Pithecophaga
  • Species :Pithecophaga jefferyi

2. Physical Characteristics

The Philippine Eagle stands 86–102 cm tall, has a wingspan of 2.0–2.2 meters, and weighs 4.5–8 kg. It has a brown body, white belly, and a distinctive golden-brown shaggy crest. Its huge bluish-gray beak and sharp talons make it an extremely powerful predator.

3. Habitat

This eagle is found only in the Philippines, particularly on the islands of Luzon, Samar, Leyte, and Mindanao. It lives in dense tropical rainforests and usually requires large territories of up to100 km² per pair due to its hunting needs.

Specialities of the Philippine Eagle

  • One of the largest and most powerful eagles in the world.
  • National bird of the Philippines, symbolizing strength and freedom.
  • Known for its unique shaggy crest resembling a lion’s mane.
  • Mates for life, making strong lifelong pair bonds.
  • Possessesextremely sharp vision capable of spotting prey from long distances.
  • Has one of the longest breeding cycles of any eagle species.
  • Considered a global symbol of biodiversity conservation.

GOLDEN PHEASAN

Golden Pheasant (Chrysolophus pictus)

1. Description

The Golden Pheasant is known for its bright and vibrant colors. Males have a golden-yellow crest, red body, green back, and an orange-and-black cape, while females are brownish for camouflage. They also have a long tail that makes up most of their length.

2. Habitat

Golden Pheasants are native to central and western China. They live in dense forests, mountains, and shrubland areas. Some populations also exist in Europe, Australia, and the United States.

3. Behavior

These birds are shy and prefer staying hidden in thick vegetation. They can run very fast and fly only short distances. Male pheasants perform beautiful courtship displays using their colorful cape and crest.

Speciality

  • One of the most colorful birds in the world.
  • The golden crest resembles a shining sun and symbolizes good luck in Chinese culture.
  • The orange cape spreads out like a lion’s mane during courtship.
  • Despite being brightly colored, it blends well with forest shadows.
